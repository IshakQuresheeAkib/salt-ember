# Salt & Ember Brand Guide

## Brand idea

Salt & Ember is where flavour meets fire: a confident, warm restaurant identity for Sylhet. The experience should feel premium and lively, never generic, overly rustic, or luxury for its own sake.

## Colour system

Use the supplied Salt & Ember logo as the source for colour grading. These are starting tokens and should be calibrated against an original transparent logo asset when available.

Use dark backgrounds as the default. Orange and red should create focus, not fill the entire interface. Maintain readable contrast; do not use amber body text on light backgrounds.

## Typography

- Display: an expressive editorial serif such as Cormorant Garamond or Playfair Display.
- Interface and body: a clean sans-serif such as Inter or DM Sans.
- The logo's script is logo-only. Do not introduce decorative script fonts throughout the interface.
- Headlines should be compact, confident, and high contrast. Body copy should be calm and generous in line height.

## Visual language

- Large food photography with close texture, flame, steam, and craft.
- Editorial asymmetry, overlapping imagery, thin amber rules, restrained grain, and rounded-but-not-bubbly surfaces.
- Food remains the hero; effects support it.
- Use dark negative space deliberately to make content and CTAs breathe.

## Avoid

- Copying the supplied inspiration's layout, components, copy, or visual identity.
- Generic orange-on-black restaurant templates.
- Excessive glassmorphism, neon, card grids, rounded pills, or decorative icons.
- Continuous flame or particle effects that compete with content.
- Low-resolution social-media logo files in production.
